# PRODUCT.md

## Product

imdbgraph — a web app that visualizes IMDb episode ratings for TV shows.
Visitors paste a show, get a season-by-season ratings view (episode block grid
default, line-chart alternative), and can click through to any episode on IMDb.

## Audience

TV fans and completionists comparing seasons; mobile and desktop.

## Voice

Terse, data-first, no marketing fluff. The data is the interface.

## Success

A visitor reads a show's rating story (which seasons peak, which collapse)
within seconds of the page loading.
